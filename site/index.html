The workspace

Everything on one screen

A full charting environment — not a widget. Up to four synchronised charts, your own layouts, and tools that persist between sessions.

19 chart types

Candles, Heikin Ashi, hollow, bars and area — plus re-binned Renko, Kagi, Point & Figure, Range and Line Break. Timeframes from 5 seconds to one week.

Drawing suite

Trend lines and channels, the full Fibonacci set with editable levels, harmonic and Elliott-wave patterns with degree labels, plus data-aware anchored VWAP.

Indicators

Moving averages, RSI, MACD, Bollinger Bands, ATR, Stochastic, VWAP, OBV, CVD and volume — each with editable parameters and colours.

Multi-chart layouts

One, two, three or four charts at once, each with its own symbol and timeframe. Save a layout — drawings included — and reopen it later.

Price alerts

Cross-above and cross-below alerts on any number of symbols at once, with browser notifications and a choice of sounds — they fire whatever chart is on screen.

Bar replay

Scrub any market back through its history and step forward bar by bar to rehearse a read without the outcome already on screen.

Derivatives

Options analysis with the exchange's own numbers

Delta publishes delta, gamma, vega, theta and open interest per contract. The chain and the gamma tools read those directly — they are not re-derived from a pricing model of our own.

Full options chain

Calls and puts side by side across every listed expiry for BTC, ETH and Gold — mark, IV, delta, 24h volume and open interest, with ITM shading and the ATM strike marked.

Gamma exposure

Call wall, put wall and gamma flip plotted on the underlying's own chart, plus a strike × expiry heatmap. A model of dealer hedging pressure — read the caveat inside.

Order flow & tape

Every print as it happens with its true aggressor side, big-trade markers, order-book imbalance, volume profile and buy/sell dominance across several timeframes at once.

Data

Straight from the exchange

The app calls Delta Exchange India's public market-data API from your browser. Nothing is relayed through a server of ours, and there is no account to connect.

  • Public endpoints only — no API key and no account required to view any market.
  • Live candles, trades and order book over WebSocket, reconnecting on their own.
  • Contract sizes normalised to base units, so volume is comparable across markets.
  • Layouts, watchlists and drawings stay in your own browser — we store nothing.
